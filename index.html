Everything is in USD. Hourly rate = monthly base ÷ (hours per week × 52 ÷ 12): 173.33 for 40 h, 130 for 30 h. An extra day and a Hero Shift are 8 h for everyone, as in the handbook; overtime 1.5x = rate × 1.5. The cleaning share is 5% of everything the Sales team sold ÷ the number of Sales people. Reimbursements are converted at the official rate on the purchase date and paid back in pesos. Each team is confirmed and printed separately. Salaries are kept in a private tab of your Sheet.
